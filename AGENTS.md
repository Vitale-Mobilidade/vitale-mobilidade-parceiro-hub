## Banco de produção — leia antes de trabalhar com Supabase

**O Supabase da Vitale é o banco integrado ao projeto Vitale Mobilidade no Lovable** (`638f4032-8ff8-45dd-b03b-e69c0c064154`). Use a conexão desse projeto no Lovable para verificar o banco, consultar dados e implantar Edge Functions. Uma conta aberta diretamente em `supabase.com` que não mostre o projeto não prova falta de acesso ao banco: confira primeiro a integração do Lovable. Não solicite outro projeto Supabase nem bloqueie a tarefa apenas por essa tela. Escritas em produção continuam sujeitas a backup, validação e autorização do escopo.

Legacy `/bikes/$slug` URLs permanently redirect to `/radar/$bikeId`; public bike discovery and detail links target Radar because it is the canonical bike destination.

Newsletter content is composed by the existing server writer and validated independently; opening policy and deterministic Radar cards belong to the content contract/renderer, never to a separate writer or database schema. This keeps generation, pricing and delivery responsibilities isolated.

Bike relevance follows [docs/BIKE_RELEVANCE_POLICY.md](docs/BIKE_RELEVANCE_POLICY.md): offer CTA clicks over the last 30 Sao Paulo calendar days, via the shared ranking RPC. Use it across Radar, Home, newsletter curation and grounded editorial context. Context, verified offers, source fidelity and rotation take precedence over popularity; never repeat sent article/video sources to fill an edition or force a popular bike into an unrelated transcript.

Independent Instagram carousels keep auth, workflow and CAS/lease checkpoints in server-only TanStack modules; podcast workers must not generate or publish social posts, so RSS release never depends on social AI or Meta.
Instagram AI calls and JPEG decode/composition run only in the editorial-admin private `instagram-internal` action (Deno Edge, ImageScript), authorized by the server-held service key plus the job lease, because Workers forbid WebAssembly compilation; the Hub bundle never imports codecs and fails closed before paid work unless Edge confirms renderer and AI. Snapshot never imports Edge/Meta eagerly.
Keep unapplied release SQL in docs/sql/instagram-release until explicitly approved and promoted by the official migration tool; protected migration journals represent applied database history and must not be bypassed.
Instagram art v2 uses a runtime-neutral intent/module contract and compact versioned product skill; the Edge renderer dispatches by artVersion so saved v1 review assets and composition remain compatible. Article originals require internal-path and catalog identity proof; otherwise use the complete real catalog photo, because generated cover association alone does not prove product fidelity.
Keep sandbox art galleries development-only with versioned review JPEGs under docs/preview, local imports behind a compile-time DEV dynamic-import branch, and no job/API actions, so private-checkout review works without CDN access and production excludes the gallery assets without weakening admin authentication.
